\section*{Running the Verifier}

\subsection*{Prerequisites and Setup}

The verifier requires Python~3.11 or later, Z3~4.13 or later (installed as the \texttt{z3-solver} PyPI package), and ANTLR~4 (available as \texttt{antlr4-python3-runtime}).

\begin{lstlisting}[style=terminal]
pip install z3-solver antlr4-python3-runtime
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection*{Python API}

\subsubsection*{Entry Point: \texttt{CHC\_Verification}}
\vspace{-2mm}
\noindent\textit{\small File: \texttt{CHC-Verification/safety\_properties.py}}

\begin{lstlisting}[style=terminal]
from safety_properties import CHC_Verification
from variable_name_detection_in_IR import OptimizationLevel

result = CHC_Verification(
    file_name,
    mode,
    user_properties,
    params            = None,
    input_ranges      = None,
    property_scope    = "all",
    pc_target         = None,
    hints             = ["check_heading_always_on_grid",
                         "check_termination"],
    timeout_ms        = 60_000,
    optimization_level= OptimizationLevel.NONE,
)
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent\textbf{Parameters.}

\begin{longtable}{p{3.2cm} p{2.0cm} p{10.0cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{file\_name} & \texttt{str} & Path to the Chiron \texttt{.tl} source file to verify. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{mode} & \texttt{str} & Verification mode. One of \texttt{"default"}, \texttt{"specific"}, or \texttt{"universal"}. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{user\_properties} & \texttt{list} & List of property objects. Each object must expose a \texttt{.name} (string label) and \texttt{.expr} (Z3-style Python expression string). Variable names match program source without the leading \texttt{:} (e.g.\ \texttt{"x >= 0"} for Chiron variable \texttt{:x}). \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{params} & \texttt{str | None} & Required only in \texttt{"specific"} mode. A string representation of a Python dictionary mapping colon-prefixed variable names to numeric values. Example: \texttt{"\{':x': 10, ':y': 5\}"}. Omitted variables default to zero. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{input\_ranges} & \texttt{dict | None} & Used only in \texttt{"universal"} mode to restrict user variables. Keys are bare variable names (no \texttt{:}). Values may be an \texttt{int} (fixed constant), a \texttt{(lo, hi)} integer interval (inclusive), or \texttt{None} (unconstrained). Unknown variable names are rejected. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{property\_scope} & \texttt{str} & One of \texttt{"all"} (default), \texttt{"terminating"}, \texttt{"at\_pc"}, or \texttt{"upto\_pc"}. Controls which reachable states the properties are checked against. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{pc\_target} & \texttt{int | None} & Required for \texttt{"at\_pc"} and \texttt{"upto\_pc"} scopes; must be an integer between 0 and the terminal PC (inclusive). Forbidden for \texttt{"all"} and \texttt{"terminating"} scopes. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{hints} & \texttt{list[str]} & Solver behaviour hints. Supplying both members of a mutually exclusive pair (prove vs.\ assume) is rejected. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{timeout\_ms} & \texttt{int} & Per-query solver timeout in milliseconds. Default: \texttt{60\_000} (60 seconds). When a query times out the corresponding property is recorded as \texttt{UNKNOWN}. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{optimization\_level} & \texttt{Optimization Level} & \texttt{OptimizationLevel.NONE} (default) uses the standard per-instruction encoding. \texttt{OptimizationLevel.BASIC} enables the turn-safety and loop-summarization pipeline. Incompatible with \texttt{property\_scope="at\_pc"} or \texttt{"upto\_pc"}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\noindent\textbf{Property objects.}

Any object with a string \texttt{.name} and a string \texttt{.expr} field is accepted; no specific class is required.
The expression in \texttt{.expr} is evaluated in a context that provides \texttt{xcor}, \texttt{ycor}, \texttt{heading}, \texttt{pendown}, \texttt{And}, \texttt{Or}, \texttt{Not}, and every user variable and loop counter by their bare name (without the \texttt{:} prefix used in Chiron source).

\begin{lstlisting}[style=terminal]
class UserProperty:
    def __init__(self, name: str, expr: str):
        self.name = name
        self.expr = expr
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent\textbf{\texttt{ReturnValue}.}

\begin{longtable}{p{3.7cm} p{2.7cm} p{9.0cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{.status} & \texttt{str} & Overall verdict: \texttt{"PASSED"}, \texttt{"FAILED"}, or \texttt{"UNKNOWN"}. \\
\texttt{.heading\_grid\_safe} & \texttt{str} & Heading grid check outcome: \texttt{"PASSED"}, \texttt{"FAILED"}, or \texttt{"UNKNOWN"}. \\
\texttt{.error} & \texttt{ReturnError} & \texttt{ReturnError.SUCCESS}, \texttt{.ERROR} (invalid input), or \texttt{.UNKNOWN}. \\
\texttt{.build\_time} & \texttt{float} & Wall-clock time (seconds) to parse and build the CHC encoding. \\
\texttt{.solve\_times} & \texttt{list[float]} & Per-property solve time in seconds, in property list order. \\
\texttt{.passing\_properties} & \texttt{list} & \texttt{[name, invariant]} pairs for each \texttt{PASSED} property. \\
\texttt{.failing\_properties} & \texttt{list} & \texttt{[name, counterexample]} pairs for each \texttt{FAILED} property. \\
\texttt{.unknown\_properties} & \texttt{list[str]} & Names of all \texttt{UNKNOWN} properties. \\
\texttt{.expr} & \texttt{str} & Human-readable summary string. \\
\texttt{.advice} & \texttt{str} & Human-readable suggestion for next steps. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsubsection*{Semantic Helper API: \texttt{CHC\_Verification\_semantic}}

\noindent\textit{\small File: \texttt{CHC-Verification/semantic\_properties.py}}

\texttt{CHC\_Verification\_semantic} wraps the core verifier with a transpilation step
that converts readable helper-style property strings into raw Z3-compatible expressions
before they reach \texttt{eval()}.

\begin{lstlisting}[style=terminal]
from semantic_properties import CHC_Verification_semantic

result = CHC_Verification_semantic(
    file_name=...,
    mode=...,
    user_properties=...,  # helper-style expressions accepted
    # all other kwargs forwarded to CHC_Verification
)
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent Property strings may use any of the helper functions listed below in
Table~\ref{tab:helpers}; raw Z3-style expressions (\texttt{x >= 0}, \texttt{And(...)})
are also accepted unchanged.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{p{5.2cm} p{2.0cm} p{7.5cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Helper} & \textbf{Args} & \textbf{Expansion} \\
\midrule
\texttt{is\_nonnegative(v)} & 1 & \texttt{v >= 0} \\
\texttt{is\_positive(v)} & 1 & \texttt{v > 0} \\
\texttt{is\_negative(v)} & 1 & \texttt{v < 0} \\
\texttt{is\_between(v, lo, hi)} & 3 & \texttt{And(v >= lo, v <= hi)} \\
\texttt{is\_strictly\_between(v, lo, hi)} & 3 & \texttt{And(v > lo, v < hi)} \\
\texttt{is\_one\_of(v, a, b, ...)} & $\ge$2 & \texttt{Or(v==a, v==b, ...)} \\
\texttt{is\_multiple\_of(v, n)} & 2 & \texttt{v \% n == 0} \\
\texttt{sum\_is(a, b, c)} & 3 & \texttt{a + b == c} \\
\texttt{pen\_is\_up()} & 0 & \texttt{Not(pendown)} \\
\texttt{pen\_is\_down()} & 0 & \texttt{pendown} \\
\texttt{x\_in\_range(lo, hi)} & 2 & \texttt{And(xcor >= lo, xcor <= hi)} \\
\texttt{y\_in\_range(lo, hi)} & 2 & \texttt{And(ycor >= lo, ycor <= hi)} \\

\subsection*{CLI Interface}

The CHC verifier is integrated into \texttt{chiron.py} and is invoked by adding \texttt{-chc} to any standard \texttt{chiron.py} command line.

\begin{lstlisting}[style=terminal]
cd Chiron-Framework/ChironCore
python chiron.py -chc [options] <program.tl>
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent\textbf{CHC-specific flags.}

\begin{longtable}{p{3.0cm} p{2.2cm} p{10.5cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Flag} & \textbf{Default} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{-chc} & --- & Enable CHC verification. Must be present to activate any of the flags below. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{-chc\_mode} & \texttt{default} & Verification mode. Choices: \texttt{default}, \texttt{specific}, \texttt{universal}. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{-chc\_props} & \textit{(none)} & Properties to verify, as a semicolon-separated list of \texttt{name:expr} pairs. \texttt{name} is a label; \texttt{expr} may be a raw Z3-style expression (e.g.\ \texttt{x >= 0}) \emph{or} a semantic helper call (e.g.\ \texttt{is\_nonnegative(x)}, \texttt{position\_in\_box(-100,100,-100,100)}); both are accepted and helper calls are transpiled automatically. Variable names omit the \texttt{:} prefix. Example: \mbox{\texttt{"x\_pos:is\_nonnegative(x);y\_bound:y <= 100"}}. Omitting this flag runs the heading-grid check only. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{-chc\_ranges} & \textit{(none)} & Input variable bounds for \texttt{universal} mode, as a Python dict literal. Keys are bare variable names (no \texttt{:}). Values may be an \texttt{int} (fixed constant), a \texttt{(lo, hi)} tuple (inclusive), or \texttt{None} (unconstrained). Example: \mbox{\texttt{-chc\_ranges '\{"x": (0, 100), "step": 1\}'}}. Only valid with \texttt{-chc\_mode universal}. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{-chc\_scope} & \texttt{all} & Property scope. Choices: \texttt{all}, \texttt{terminating}, \texttt{at\_pc}, \texttt{upto\_pc}. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{-chc\_pc} & \textit{(none)} & Target PC (integer) for \texttt{at\_pc} or \texttt{upto\_pc} scope. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{-chc\_timeout} & \texttt{60000} & Solver timeout per query in milliseconds. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{-chc\_hints} & \textit{default hint} & Comma-separated solver hints. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{-chc\_opt} & \texttt{NONE} & Encoding optimization level. Choices: \texttt{NONE}, \texttt{BASIC}. \texttt{BASIC} incompatible with \texttt{at\_pc}/\texttt{upto\_pc} scope. \\
\addlinespace
\texttt{-d} / \texttt{--params} & \textit{(none)} & Initial variable values for \texttt{specific} mode, as a Python dict string. Example: \texttt{-d '\{":x": 10, ":y": 5\}'}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsubsection*{CLI Output}

After the solver completes, \texttt{chiron.py} prints a structured result block:

\begin{lstlisting}[style=terminal]
=== CHC Verification Result ===
Status          : PASSED
Heading-grid    : PASSED
Build time      : 0.031s
Passing         : ['x_nonneg']
Message         : All properties satisfied.
Advice          : The program is safe to run with respect to the specified properties.
\end{lstlisting}
